Explainer · 2-col + support card

What Is Cloud Accounting and How Does It Work?

Cloud accounting is the use of online accounting software to manage your business finances. Instead of records on a desktop, spreadsheet or paper system, your information is stored securely online and accessed when needed.

Cloud software connects with bank feeds, invoices, receipts and reporting tools — reducing manual data entry and giving a more up-to-date view of your financial position.

It also makes collaboration easier: your accountant works with the same information, helping resolve issues, prepare reports and keep records organised without passing files back and forth. Platforms such as Xero and QuickBooks are commonly used.

FAQ · accordion + FAQ schema

Frequently Asked Questions

What is cloud accounting? –
The use of online accounting software to manage financial records, bookkeeping, invoicing and reporting — letting business owners and accountants access information through a secure online platform.
Is Xero better than QuickBooks? +
Both are popular cloud platforms. The best choice depends on your business, how you manage invoices and expenses, what reporting you need and how you prefer to work. An accountant can help you choose.
What is Making Tax Digital? +
An HMRC initiative requiring many businesses to keep digital records and use compatible software for certain tax reporting. Cloud accounting software helps maintain digital records more efficiently.
Do I need cloud accounting for my business? +
It's useful if you want better access to information, less manual admin, easier collaboration with your accountant and more organised digital records — especially for growing businesses and those preparing for MTD.
How does cloud accounting help SMEs? +
By improving visibility, reducing manual data entry, supporting faster reporting and making records easier to manage all year — helping owners make better decisions and stay organised.
