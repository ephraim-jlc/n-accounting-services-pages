What Is Bookkeeping?

Bookkeeping is the process of recording and organising your business’s daily financial transactions, entailing everything from income, expenses, invoices and receipts, to bank payments and activity.

Accurate bookkeeping helps ensure your accounts are based on reliable information. It supports tax returns, VAT records, annual accounts, management accounts and general financial reporting.

For small businesses, organised records give you a clearer view of profitability, costs, tax readiness and available cash. Modern bookkeeping often uses cloud software such as Xero or QuickBooks, helping keep records up to date and aligned with Making Tax Digital.

FAQ · accordion + FAQ schema

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a bookkeeper do? –
A bookkeeper records and organises a business’s financial transactions — income, expenses, invoices, receipts, bank reconciliation — keeping records ready for accounts and tax reporting.
How much do bookkeeping services cost in the UK? +
It depends on business size, transaction volume, software used and support required. Many small businesses choose monthly packages so costs are predictable.
Do small businesses need bookkeeping software? +
Software makes it easier to keep records organised, especially with digital record keeping and Making Tax Digital. Xero or QuickBooks can help with invoices, bank feeds, expenses and reporting.
Is outsourcing bookkeeping worth it? +
It can be, for small businesses that want to save time, improve accuracy and avoid falling behind with admin. It's often more cost-effective than hiring in-house.
What software is best for bookkeeping? +
It depends on your business size, processes and reporting needs. Popular options include Xero and QuickBooks, but the right choice depends on how you manage invoices, expenses, transactions and tax records.
