Professional Holding Company Setup for Growing Businesses

As a business becomes more profitable, the way it is structured starts to matter. Cash builds up in the trading company, new ventures appear, and owners begin to think about protecting what they have built and planning for the future.

A holding company structure places a parent company above your trading business. Done properly, it can give you more flexibility over how profits are held and reinvested, and separate valuable assets from day-to-day trading risk.

N-Accounting manages the whole process for you, from the initial plan and valuation through to incorporation and the share exchange paperwork, so the restructure is handled correctly from the start.

What Is a Holding Company and How Does It Work?

A holding company is a limited company that owns the shares in one or more other companies, known as subsidiaries. You own the holding company, and the holding company owns your trading business.

For an existing business, the structure is usually put in place through a share-for-share exchange: you swap your shares in the trading company for shares in the new holding company. The trading company carries on as normal, with the same customers, staff, contracts and bank account.

Once the group is in place, profits can be moved up to the holding company and held, invested or used to fund other ventures. The right structure depends on your circumstances, so advice should always come before any restructure.

A Holding Structure Can Help With

  • Separating surplus cash from trading risk
  • Protecting valuable assets and property
  • Moving profits between group companies
  • Funding new ventures or investments
  • Running several businesses under one group
  • Planning for a future sale or exit
  • Succession and bringing in new shareholders

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a holding company? –
A holding company is a limited company that owns the shares in one or more other companies, known as subsidiaries. It usually doesn't trade itself. Instead it holds shares, cash, property or other assets on behalf of the group.
How much does it cost to set up a holding company? +
Our holding company setup is offered for a pre-agreed fixed fee of £500 – £1,500. This doesn't include a formal HMRC clearance application, stamp duty or Companies House filing fees. More complex group structures are quoted separately.
What is a share-for-share exchange? +
It is the step that places the holding company above your existing business. You exchange your shares in the trading company for shares in the new holding company, so you own the holding company and it owns the trading company.
Do I need HMRC clearance to set up a holding company? +
Not always. Advance clearance gives certainty over how HMRC will treat the transaction, and whether it is worthwhile depends on your circumstances. We advise on this as part of the service. A formal clearance application is available at an additional cost.
Will setting up a holding company affect my trading business? +
Your trading company continues as the same legal entity, so day-to-day operations carry on as normal. What changes is the ownership above it. We'll explain any practical steps, such as updating company records, as part of your plan.
Is this service suitable for property rental businesses? +
No. This service is designed for businesses whose main activity is the selling of goods or services. It is not suitable where the main activity is the rental of property.
