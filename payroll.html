How Does Payroll and PAYE work in the UK?

Payroll is the process of calculating and managing employee pay. It involves managing wages and salaries, tax deductions, National Insurance contributions, payslips and records of payments made to employees.

PAYE (Pay As You Earn) is the system HMRC uses to collect Income Tax and National Insurance from employees through payroll. Employers are responsible for calculating the correct deductions and reporting payroll information to HMRC.

For small businesses, payroll directly affects employees, cash flow and compliance. Accurate payroll helps staff get paid correctly, keeps HMRC records up to date and reduces the risk of errors or missed submissions.

HMRC Compliance and RTI Reporting

Payroll is closely linked to HMRC compliance. Employers need to make sure payroll information is accurate, submitted on time and aligned with PAYE reporting requirements.

RTI (Real Time Information) is the system used to report payroll details to HMRC. Payroll information must be submitted regularly and accurately, rather than being dealt with only at year end.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PAYE? –
PAYE stands for Pay As You Earn. It is the system used by HMRC to collect Income Tax and National Insurance from employees through payroll.
How does payroll work in the UK? +
Payroll involves calculating employee pay, deducting tax and National Insurance, preparing payslips and reporting payroll information to HMRC. Employers need to make sure it's accurate and submitted correctly.
Do I need payroll services for my small business? +
If you employ staff, payroll services help you manage PAYE, payslips, deductions and HMRC reporting. Many small businesses outsource payroll to save time and reduce the risk of errors.
How much do payroll services cost in the UK? +
It depends on the number of employees, the complexity of payroll and the support required. Many small businesses choose monthly packages so costs are predictable.
What is RTI reporting? +
RTI stands for Real Time Information — the system used to report payroll information to HMRC. Employers usually submit payroll information each time employees are paid.
